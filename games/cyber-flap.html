How to Play Cyber Flap

Cyber Flap is a high-precision arcade game. Tap or press the Spacebar to trigger micro-bursts of rocket thrust. Gravity pulls your drone down continuously.

Pro Tip: Keep your taps short and rhythmic to maintain a stable horizontal hover altitude.
