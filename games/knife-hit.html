    How to Play Cyber Knife Hit

    Tap your screen or hit Space to fire blades into the spinning core. Each successful hit embeds a knife. If your knife hits another blade, it shatters and ends the game.

    Pro Tip: Watch the target's rotation pattern—it accelerates, slows down, and occasionally reverses direction!
